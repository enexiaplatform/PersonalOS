# Henry OS
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Henry, a Vietnamese sales manager, working across full-time employment, Servicebio preparation, personal ventures, Enexia and personal development. Desktop is the main data workspace; phone access must retain navigation and task details.

## Product Purpose
See weekly work by group, inspect source-based progress, access commercial research and product evidence, and decide the next action.

## Operating Context
Notion is the weekly source of truth. OneNote supplies annual objectives. Authenticated Personal OS contains Servicebio, Ventures and Enexia modules. Calendar edits enter a Notion verification queue.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Keep every domain, menu item, route, source record and permission unchanged. Existing static web app with private HTML modules. Shared design layer may change visual hierarchy and density, not product facts or completion semantics. Do not invent revenue, completion percentages or evidence.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve apps/personal-os-v1/henry-os-mark.png byte for byte. Its interwoven lavender, jade and bronze ribbons are the visual origin. User explicitly delegates the replacement design system and rejects slogans, oversized headers, and text-heavy decorative panels.

## Product Principles
- Task completion and comprehension over decoration.
- Sources and uncertainty remain visible where they affect interpretation.
- One consistent visual vocabulary across domains.
- Vietnamese text must render correctly.

## Evidence on Hand
Current source, live private modules and user screenshots. No new business claims are authorized by a design request.
